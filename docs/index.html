Heavy wallpaper apps are over.

No Electron shell, no transcoding, no web view. Your GPU plays the video directly — nothing in between.

ENGINE

Hardware-decoded, direct to the layer

AVPlayer feeds AVPlayerLayer with no filters and no re-encoding. HEVC H.265 plays with near-zero CPU on Apple Silicon.

DISPLAYS

One panel per screen

The loop follows your setup — built-in Retina, external QHD, whatever arrangement you run. Change monitors and it rebuilds itself.

BATTERY

Muted, and it knows when to stop

Starts muted for the lowest CPU draw. Auto-pauses when an app goes fullscreen, when Low Power Mode kicks in, and on the lock screen. Resumes after.

MEMORY

Your video, remembered

A security-scoped bookmark keeps your file through reboots. Pick it once; add the app to Login Items and never think about it again.

Live in three moves.

1

Download & open

Unzip, drop LiveWallpaper.app anywhere. First launch: right-click → Open.

2

Pick your video

Menu bar → Choose 4K Video… — .mp4 or .mov, stays where it is, no copying.

3

Forget it

It loops on every display from now on. Pause and volume live in the menu bar.
